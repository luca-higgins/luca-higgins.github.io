BuilderBot & BattleBots

Modular combat robot subsystems and structural components for a 6-DOF robotic arm.

TimelineFebruary 2026 – present
TeamBuilderBot and BattleBots, Stanford Student Robotics Club

My Role & Contribution

I design and fabricate modular combat robot subsystems with hot-swappable drive and weapon assemblies for rapid repairs during competition. I also develop structural components for a 6-DOF robotic arm using CAD and additive manufacturing, collaborating across mechanical, electrical, and software teams to integrate ROS 2-controlled hardware with AI-based visuomotor control.
