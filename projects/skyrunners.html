SkyRunners VTOL Drone

Mechanical engineering lead for an autonomous fixed-wing drone delivery platform.

TimelineSeptember 2024 – present
TeamSkyRunners, Stanford Student Robotics Club
Linksstanforduav.org

Objective

Create an autonomous delivery drone platform, balancing variables like autonomy, ground station infrastructure, and operational efficiency.

My Role & Contribution

I led mechanical engineering for the platform and designed vertiports to interface with both human operators and the drone itself. I designed and manufactured custom mechanical components through iterative prototyping and additive manufacturing, and contributed to flight testing, systems integration, and presentation of the Mission Concept Review.
