Stanford Student Robotics

RoboSub AUV

Designing and fabricating mechanical components for an autonomous underwater vehicle.

TimelineSeptember 2024 – March 2026
TeamRoboSub Competition Team, Stanford Student Robotics Club
Linksstanfordrobosub.org

Objective

Create an autonomous submersible robot capable of competing in the RoboSub competition, with a secondary research thread on kelp forest monitoring in partnership with MBARI.

My Role & Contribution

I designed and manufactured custom mechanical components for the vehicle, primarily 3D modeling and fabricating parts for the electronics enclosure to maximize access and organization. I developed CAD models, fabricated components using additive manufacturing, and iterated designs to improve reliability in underwater environments.
