About

Hi, I'm Luca.

Mechanical engineering undergraduate at Stanford University, based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Stanford University (Class of 2028) with a focus on robotics, mechanics, and fabrication. Since February 2025, I've been an undergraduate research assistant in the Soft Intelligent Materials Laboratory (PI: Professor Renee Zhao), where I develop magnetically actuated milli-scale robots for minimally invasive thrombectomy — combining computational fluid dynamics, rapid prototyping, and experimental testing.

Outside the lab, I'm heavily involved with Stanford Student Robotics, a 150+ member student organization. I served as Co-President from May 2025 to June 2026, previously as Financial Officer, and I contribute mechanical engineering work across the SkyRunners VTOL drone team, the RoboSub autonomous submersible team, and the BuilderBot/BattleBots combat robotics team.

I'm drawn to problems where fundamental mechanics — buckling, actuation, materials behavior — determine whether a system actually works outside of simulation. I like taking a project from a CAD model to a tested, working prototype.
